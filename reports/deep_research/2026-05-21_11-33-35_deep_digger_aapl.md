## 8. Bull Case

* Sustained gross margin resilience, supported by efficient supply chain management and premium pricing

## 9. Bear Case

* China demand slowdown, impacting hardware sales and revenue growth
* Intensifying competition in the smartphone and personal computer markets, eroding market share and pricing power
* Failure to innovate and adapt to changing consumer preferences, leading to declining sales and revenue
